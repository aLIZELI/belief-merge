## A. Belief revision & merging

- **On the Logic of Theory Change: Partial Meet Contraction and Revision Functions** (Alchourrón, Gärdenfors, Makinson / *Journal of Symbolic Logic* 50(2):510–530, 1985) — DOI:10.2307/2274239 — [https://doi.org/10.2307/2274239] — AGM: rational contraction/revision characterized by partial-meet contraction plus the Levi identity (the eight postulates).
- **Merging Information Under Constraints: A Logical Framework** (Konieczny, Pino Pérez / *Journal of Logic and Computation* 12(5):773–808, 2002) — DOI:10.1093/logcom/12.5.773 — [https://doi.org/10.1093/logcom/12.5.773] — Integrity-constraint-based family of merging operators (majority, arbitration, egalitarian) with IC postulates.
- **On the Logic of Merging** (Konieczny, Pino Pérez / *KR 1998 — Principles of Knowledge Representation and Reasoning*, pp. 488–498, 1998) — [https://openalex.org/W163597783] — First logical characterization of merging via "syncretic"/arbitration assignments and the IC0–IC8 postulates.
- **On the Logic of Iterated Belief Revision** (Darwiche, Pearl / *Artificial Intelligence* 89(1–2):1–29, 1997) — DOI:10.1016/S0004-3702(96)00038-0 — [https://doi.org/10.1016/S0004-3702(96)00038-0] — DP postulates (C1–C4) governing how a revision operator behaves under sequences of revisions.
- **Iterated Belief Change Based on Epistemic Entrenchment** (Nayak / *Erkenntnis* 41, 1994) — DOI:10.1007/BF01130759 — [https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01130759] — Iterated revision driven by epistemic entrenchment orderings.
- **Dynamic Belief Revision Operators** (Nayak, Pagnucco, Peppas / *Artificial Intelligence* 146(2):193–228, 2003) — DOI:10.1016/S0004-3702(03)00017-1 — [https://doi.org/10.1016/S0004-3702(03)00017-1] — Revision operators that respect the *dynamics* of the belief state, not just the current belief set.
- **Iterated Belief Revision, Revised** (Jin, Thielscher / *Artificial Intelligence* 171(1):1–18, 2007) — DOI:10.1016/j.artint.2006.11.002 — [https://doi.org/10.1016/j.artint.2006.11.002] — Replaces the DP postulates with a set justified by a concrete semantic construction (lexicographic revision).
- **Admissible and Restrained Revision** (Booth, Meyer / *Journal of Artificial Intelligence Research* 26:127–151, 2006) — DOI:10.1613/jair.1874 — [https://doi.org/10.1613/jair.1874] — "Restrained" revision satisfies the DP postulates while keeping revision admissible.
- **Investigations into a Theory of Knowledge Base Revision: Preliminary Report** (Dalal / *AAAI 1988*, pp. 475–479) — [https://openalex.org/W2160579271] — Dalal distance = number of propositional atoms on which two models differ; revise to a minimum-distance model.
- **Nonmonotonic Reasoning by Minimal Belief Revision** (Satoh / *FGCS 1988 — Fifth Generation Computer Systems*, 1988) — [https://openalex.org/W68974267] — Reads nonmonotonic consequence as set-inclusion-minimal change of a belief set.
- **Propositional Knowledge Base Revision and Minimal Change** (Katsuno, Mendelzon / *Artificial Intelligence* 52(3):263–294, 1991) — DOI:10.1016/0004-3702(91)90069-V — [https://doi.org/10.1016/0004-3702(91)90069-V] — KM postulates; revision/update defined via total preorders over interpretations.
- *Recent:* **Collective Belief Revision** (Aravanis / *Journal of Artificial Intelligence Research* 78:1221–1247, 2023) — DOI:10.1613/jair.1.15745 — [https://doi.org/10.1613/jair.1.15745] — Extends AGM-style revision to belief sets held by *groups* of agents.

## B. Truth maintenance systems

- **A Truth Maintenance System** (Doyle / *Artificial Intelligence* 12(3):231–272, 1979) — DOI:10.1016/0004-3702(79)90008-0 — [https://doi.org/10.1016/0004-3702(79)90008-0] — JTMS: a network of justifications that keeps the current belief set consistent under additions/retractions.
- **Truth Maintenance Systems for Problem Solving** (Doyle / MIT AI Lab Technical Report TR-419, 1977) — [https://openalex.org/W1488207627] — Precursor report that introduced dependency-directed (non-monotonic) belief maintenance.
- **An Assumption-Based TMS** (de Kleer / *Artificial Intelligence* 28(2):127–162, 1986) — DOI:10.1016/0004-3702(86)90080-9 — [https://doi.org/10.1016/0004-3702(86)90080-9] — ATMS maintains *all* consistent assumption environments (labels/nogoods) instead of a single belief set.
- **Extending the ATMS** (de Kleer / *Artificial Intelligence* 28(2):163–196, 1986) — DOI:10.1016/0004-3702(86)90081-0 — [https://doi.org/10.1016/0004-3702(86)90081-0] — Label-update algorithms, nogood handling and AR/AR-context efficiency for the ATMS.
- **Foundations of Assumption-Based Truth Maintenance Systems: Preliminary Report** (Reiter, de Kleer / *AAAI 1987*) — [https://openalex.org/W145128706] — Gives the ATMS a logical foundation (environments, nogoods) rather than a procedural one.
- **Building Problem Solvers** (Forbus, de Kleer / MIT Press, 1993) — ISBN 9780262528153 — [https://mitpress.mit.edu/9780262528153/building-problem-solvers/] — Book-length treatment of TMS/ATMS engines and their use in search/constraint problem solving.
- **An Outlook on Truth Maintenance** (McAllester / MIT AI Memo 551, 1980) — [http://hdl.handle.net/1721.1/6327] — Ties truth maintenance to dependency-directed reasoning; early statement of the "justification" view.

## C. Non-monotonic / defeasible reasoning

- **Defeasible Logic** (Nute / *Handbook of Logic in Artificial Intelligence and Logic Programming, Vol. 3*, Oxford University Press, pp. 353–395, 1994) — DOI:10.1093/oso/9780198537472.003.0007 — [https://doi.org/10.1093/oso/9780198537472.003.0007] — Rule-based defeasible reasoning with strict/defeasible rules and a superiority relation.
- **Defeasible Reasoning** (Pollock / *Cognitive Science* 11(4):481–518, 1987) — DOI:10.1207/s15516709cog1104_4 — [https://doi.org/10.1207/s15516709cog1104_4] — Defeasible reasons plus rebutting/undercutting defeaters; implemented in the OSCAR architecture.
- **Nonmonotonic Reasoning: An Overview** (Brewka, Dix, Konolige / CSLI Lecture Notes 73, 1997) — [https://openalex.org/W1569596742] — Textbook survey of default logic, circumscription, autoepistemic logic and their relationships.
- **On the Acceptability of Arguments and Its Fundamental Role in Nonmonotonic Reasoning, Logic Programming and n-Person Games** (Dung / *Artificial Intelligence* 77(2):321–357, 1995) — DOI:10.1016/0004-3702(94)00041-X — [https://doi.org/10.1016/0004-3702(94)00041-X] — Abstract argumentation frameworks; admissible/complete/grounded/stable extensions as a unifying semantics.
- *Recent:* **On the Acceptability of Arguments … : 25 Years Later** (Baroni, Toni, Verheij / *Argument & Computation* 11(1–2):1–14, 2020) — DOI:10.3233/AAC-200901 — [https://doi.org/10.3233/AAC-200901] — Retrospective on the impact and open problems of Dung-style argumentation.

## D. Paraconsistent logic

- **The Logic of Paradox** (Priest / *Journal of Philosophical Logic* 8(1):219–241, 1979) — DOI:10.1007/BF00258428 — [https://doi.org/10.1007/BF00258428] — LP admits truth-value *gluts* (both true and false) so contradictions do not trivialize inference.
- **On the Theory of Inconsistent Formal Systems** (da Costa / *Notre Dame Journal of Formal Logic* 15(4):497–510, 1974) — DOI:10.1305/ndjfl/1093891487 — [https://doi.org/10.1305/ndjfl/1093891487] — The paraconsistent hierarchy C_n; inconsistency is localized rather than explosive.
- **A Useful Four-Valued Logic** (Belnap / in *Modern Uses of Multiple-Valued Logic*, pp. 5–37, 1977) — DOI:10.1007/978-94-010-1161-7_2 — [https://doi.org/10.1007/978-94-010-1161-7_2] — Four values {T, F, both, neither} on a bilattice; the standard "useful" paraconsistent semantics.
- **The Paraconsistent Logics PJ** (da Costa, Subrahmanian, Vago / *Mathematical Logic Quarterly* 37(9–12):139–148, 1991) — DOI:10.1002/malq.19910370903 — [https://doi.org/10.1002/malq.19910370903] — Annotated (PJ) paraconsistent logic used to reason about inconsistent knowledge bases.
- **Introduction to Annotated Logics: Foundations for Paracomplete and Paraconsistent Reasoning** (Abe, Akama, Nakamatsu / Springer, 2015) — DOI:10.1007/978-3-319-17912-4 — [https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-319-17912-4] — Book defining annotated logics (each formula carries a lattice annotation).
- **A Survey of Inconsistency-Adaptive Logics** (Batens / in *Frontiers of Paraconsistent Logic*, pp. 49–73, 2000) — [https://users.ugent.be/~dbatens/publ/A2000D_surv-ial.pdf] — Adaptive logics that treat inconsistencies as "abnormalities" and dynamically revise which formulas are derivable.
- **A Universal Logic Approach to Adaptive Logics** (Batens / *Logica Universalis* 1(1):221–242, 2007) — DOI:10.1007/s11787-006-0012-5 — [https://doi.org/10.1007/s11787-006-0012-5] — General schema (lower limit logic + abnormality + strategy) generating all adaptive logics.
- *Recent:* **A Comprehensive Review on Paraconsistent Annotated Evidential Logic: Algorithms, Applications, and Perspectives** (de Carvalho Junior, Justo, de Oliveira, da Silva Filho / *Engineering Applications of Artificial Intelligence* 127:107342, 2024) — DOI:10.1016/j.engappai.2023.107342 — [https://doi.org/10.1016/j.engappai.2023.107342] — Review of the Eτ annotated paraconsistent logic and its engineering applications.

## I. Incremental view maintenance (and DB truth maintenance)
